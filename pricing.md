Tier list. All amounts in EUR, net of VAT. EU B2B reverse-charge applies for validated EU VAT-registered customers (Art. 196 Dir. 2006/112/EC); out-of-scope for non-EU customers.

## Refund policy

- Subscription tiers: cancel any time through the customer portal; cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period. No partial-period refunds.
- If a paid feature is materially broken (functional regression we introduced), the affected billing period is refunded in full on request.
- One-time consulting engagements: cancellation before kickoff = 100% refund; after kickoff before deliverable = 50%; after deliverable sent = no refund.

## Currency

EUR only. GBP / USD on request via Wise, with conversion fees borne by the buyer.

## VAT

B2B EU reverse-charge for validated EU VATIN; out-of-scope for non-EU; deferred for Spanish-resident customers pending autónomo registration.
